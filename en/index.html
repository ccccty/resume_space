About

About me

My research focuses on fusion of optical and SAR satellite imagery and on real-time inference over compute-constrained platforms in space computing scenarios. The regime differs from ground vision: acquisition is uncontrolled, with cloud, terrain shadow and speckle acting at once; labelled samples are scarce and extrapolate poorly; and on-board or in-orbit platforms are bounded in power, compute and downlink bandwidth. My approach is to split the interpretation chain into independently verifiable stages — modality harmonisation and registration, sample generation, fusion network, deployment optimisation — each closed by a measurable accuracy criterion.

At Huantian Wisdom — the operator of the Tianfu (Huantian) constellation of joint optical and SAR remote sensing satellites — I worked on L1/L2 image product processing and multi-source fusion analysis, delivering farmland semantic segmentation, parcel-level crop extraction and quantitative assessment for geological and agricultural insurance claims. At SAST I now lead a multi-source fusion refactor bringing point cloud, infrared and visible-light data into a unified framework, and I am familiar with the full path from model training and migration onto domestic compute platforms through to post-launch quality maintenance.

Satellite Application · Live Jun 2024 – Feb 2025

Xingkan Heshan: Farmland Interpretation and Claim Quantification

A remote sensing application for provincial high-standard farmland supervision and insurance loss assessment. On the data side, L1/L2 products are generated from Tianfu constellation optical and SAR imagery — radiometric calibration and system geometric correction, orthorectification and atmospheric correction, multi-spectral pan-sharpening and mosaicking — with sub-pixel cross-modal registration driven by point-and-line features and control points, yielding a base layer on a unified spatial reference. On the application side, joint optical and SAR observation replaces single-source data, covering optical gaps in cloudy regions and parcel boundaries obscured by cloud. Parcels are vectorised into a common map on which crop type, planted area and growth grade are reported; for damage events, pre- and post-event imagery are compared to quantify the affected extent and area as auditable claim evidence.

Remote Sensing Simulation · Open Source 2026

RGB / SAR / LiDAR Multimodal Remote Sensing Dataset Simulator

A multimodal sensing simulator addressing the scarcity of labelled remote sensing training data. Given a 3D model and an observation geometry, it generates visible, infrared (MWIR / LWIR), millimetre-wave radar range–Doppler, stripmap SAR (range–Doppler focusing, speckle and NESZ) and LiDAR point-cloud data offline. All modalities share one 3D world and one weather state, labels are projected from a single set of 3D boxes, so cross-modal pixel-level registration is inherent and datasets export directly to YOLO and COCO. Pure Python (numpy plus headless Blender), no GPU and no network required, with a parameterised studio and an online dashboard.
